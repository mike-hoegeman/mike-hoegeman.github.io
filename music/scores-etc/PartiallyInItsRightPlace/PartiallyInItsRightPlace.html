This piece began by me coming across a youtube video that had a piano player playing a short chord sequence of chords in a Radiohead song. I liked the way the sequence 'moved through the common tone C' from a root I chord C Major to a IV F Major chord. So, I entered the sequence into musescore just to experiment with it a bit.

I not familiar with radiohead really, and I didn't even know the name of the song (at first), which turned out to be Everything In Its Right Place.

There is some debate as to what the key signature is of the original song. My construction does not have much relation to the orignal source. As a result, I just left the (initial) notated key signature as C Major
