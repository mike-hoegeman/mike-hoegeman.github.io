mike hoegeman | music | ghosts (dairy farm processional)

This page is a guide to learning the piece Ghosts on a tapping Instrument such as the Chapman Stick or the acoustic Dragonfly DFA. This piece would work pretty well on piano also or as a duo with a wind instrument with a bit of arranging.

There is a ♪ score/tablature ♪ and fretboard diagrams to help you learn the song.

This re-imagining of the melody from 'Ghosts' by Albert Ayler has a more straight up, simple rhythm than the original jazz classic and has an americana feel with the use of a slower tempo.

Some spare bass accompanyment with a few different chords here and there makes it a different feeling song all together.
