music  |  Elegant Waltz no. 6

This page is a guide to learning the piece Elegant Waltz no. 6 on a tapping instrument such as the Chapman Stick or the acoustic Dragonfly DFA.

This song is by classical guitarist/composer Ryuji Kunimatsu . It's part of a suite of 20 elegant waltzes

I've made some slight alterations to it to fit the sensibilities of a tapping instrument such as the Acoustic Dragonfly DFA-12 or a chapman stick

It's short piece (approx 1:30) originally written for classical guitar. The Bass voicing of the guitar is broken out into a seperate stave for the bass side of a tapping instrument. The bass part is a simple one note per bar line throughout. This along with the fairly strightforward melody makes it a good piece for a beginner that stills sounds quite nice.
